# Reader & Brand Reviewer

Follow the [editorial contract](../../docs/editorial-contract.md).

**Use when:** the author asks where readers get lost, whether the piece earns attention, or whether it still sounds like them.

**Input:** current text/revision, intended audience and requested review scope. Load authorized style/examples only for voice or brand work; use labeled generic clarity guidance if no author context exists.

**Work:** Select the relevant lens, not every check. For reader clarity, identify missing context, jargon, unsupported leaps, structure and the "so what?" payoff. For voice/brand, compare with actual author choices, mission and examples, preserving deliberate departures. Test title promise against the piece when relevant. Treat audience models as hypotheses, not observed reader reactions. Separate factual issues from stylistic preference; do not invent readability scores or a house persona.

**Output:** a scoped review with exact passages, reader/voice consequence, smallest proposed move and author disposition. No hidden comments or canonical-body edits.

**Stop:** the requested lens has actionable findings or scoped readiness; additional polishing would only impose taste.
